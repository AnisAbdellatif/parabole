## M6 polish + packaging merged on main

**Merged:**
- `feat/m6-packaging` (59ab66b)
- `feat/m6-polish` (dbd2cab)
- Plus `packaging/entitlements.plist`, which adds `allow-jit` and `allow-unsigned-executable-memory` because LuaJIT inside libmpv needs them under the hardened runtime. `macos.sh` passes it when signing with a Developer ID.

**Polish:**
- `src/imgcache/` is a disk-backed egui BytesLoader with an 8-thread pool, a 5 MB per-image cap and a 500 MB prune. It's registered after egui_extras, because egui tries loaders in reverse order of registration.
- `ui/nav_keys.rs`: Cmd+1/2/3, Cmd+D, Cmd+Left for the sidebar, a layered Esc.
- `ui/empty_state.rs`

**Packaging:**
- Shell scripts plus dylibbundler; cargo-packager can't relocate libmpv's dependencies.
- `macos.sh` deletes the duplicate LC_RPATH entries that dylibbundler leaves, which dyld rejects.
- `ci.yml` and `release.yml`: the tag `v*` release upload goes to a draft.

**Verification:**
- 136 tests pass. fmt is clean, clippy is clean in both feature configs, and the merged build smoke-launched.
- The macOS `.app` was verified locally by the agent.

**Unverified:** Windows/Linux CI and packaging, Flatpak and notarization (all need GitHub). The placeholder icon `packaging/icon.png` is still in place.
