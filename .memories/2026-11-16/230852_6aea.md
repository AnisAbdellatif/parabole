## IMDb/Trakt direction approved by user

- **Phase 1:** IMDb details. They are fetched when a details or series view opens, cached in `media_links` + `media_meta` (migration 12), and fall back to the Trakt public API. The setting is on by default.
- **Phase 2:** link Trakt with the device flow, client_id only. Push only: scrobble from the embedded player, plus manual watched marks going to `/sync/history`.
- **Phase 3 (pull):** postponed.
- **Trakt client_id:** the user will register the app and provide it. Until then `trakt::CLIENT_ID` is an empty const, and Trakt calls bail with "Trakt is not configured".
