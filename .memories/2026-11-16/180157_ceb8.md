## Smart tab + measured stream quality (feat/smart-tab, worktree ../parabole-smart)

- **What:** `stream_quality` table (migration_7, keyed by source_id+ext_key because stream ids change on refresh), recorded by embedded player 10s after first frame; failures counted on give-up. Badges on Live rows. Smart tab (rail index 3, `Persisted.smart`, `state.tab` stays Live) groups live streams by `alt_name::normalize`, ranks variants (no failures > effective height > measured > fps > startup), sidebar = exclusive quality bands + 50/60 fps. Fallback on failure: next variant in channel, then cross-source alternates.
